## Inputs

All 80 user prompts and controlled authority contexts are project-authored,
non-private research fixtures. They contain no real user chat, Character
Memory, runtime Progress, device logs, third-party dialogue, social-media
text, or identity data.

loopback-only reference `llama.cpp` runtime. They are classified as
`project_authored_prompt_local_qwen_output_local_only` for this Pilot.

Generated reply text remains outside Git. This Pilot does not grant public
redistribution approval for generated text, the Qwen GGUF, checkpoints, ONNX
artifacts, or vocabularies.
